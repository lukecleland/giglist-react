## DKNG — Outside Lands identity
Source and portfolio: https://www.dkngstudios.com/work/festivals
Inspected the 2014 Outside Lands shirt illustration linked from that page: paired trees frame compact dimensional lettering. Restrained flat colours and hatch detail make illustration and type feel like one object. Adapt mirrored organic ornament and a limited palette for acoustic/folk bills; do not reuse the illustration.

## Implemented original directions
- Stained glass: radial colour fragments, perimeter diamonds and a dark central cartouche; Sancreek with outline/shadow.
- Western engraving: double rules, diamond cuts and corner rosettes; Rye with a restrained shadow.
- Botanical folk: mirrored leaf sprigs and delicate rules; a plain expressive serif.
- Sunburst print: flat radial rays around a central title field; rounded geometric display lettering with offset/outline treatment.

These are compact reusable banners, not replicas of full festival identities. Future directions should change illustration and composition as well as typography. Keep header artwork small enough that a dense listing remains useful at print size.

## Expanded full-colour artwork
