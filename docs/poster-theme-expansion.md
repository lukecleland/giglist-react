# Additional live-music poster themes

Added 20 themes (26 total), using the existing licensed photo/art library and local fonts.

## Design references

- [Cooper Hewitt: Jazz Motions](https://www.cooperhewitt.org/2018/05/29/jazz-motions/): contrasting colours and rhythmic stripes inform colour rails and edge marks.
- [Cooper Hewitt: Art of Noise](https://www.cooperhewitt.org/channel/art-of-noise/): music graphics range from photographic sleeves to photocopied flyers; retain a mix of intimate and energetic designs.
- [V&A poster collection](https://www.vam.ac.uk/collections/posters): poster framing and strong typographic hierarchy inform framed-image compositions.

References inform composition only; their images are not distributed. Source photos and image permissions are in `src/assets/posters/SOURCES.md`.

## Added themes

Fireside, Soft Focus, The Parlour, Sunday Paper, Slow Coast, Amber Room,
After Hours, Blue Note, Velvet, Contact Sheet, Signal, Night Drive, Daybreak,
Field Notes, Desert Bloom, Moonlit, Cut & Paste, Loud Type, Evergreen and Ultraviolet.

Ten composition families cover sleeve photography, framed windows, double-rule
frames, mastheads, panoramic horizons, asymmetric splits, colour rails, ticket
borders, full-image covers and photographic collages. Paired themes vary their
placement/alignment, typography, photographic treatment and image pools.

Dense variants retain full-page images under protective text panels. All gigs
are preserved and fitted into the available area. Real venue photos take
priority, font shuffle remains independent, and each theme retains the common
left QR (35% above the footer) and right-aligned Giglist signature.

Validation: rendered all 26 themes with nine gigs, 92 gigs and venue imagery;
100 renderer/page tests passed, including 200 long listings in every theme.
